About Meet at Event

Personal event planning, joyful movement, and celebrations made memorable.

OUR APPROACH

We manage and host your event

Meet at Event brings planning, coordination, and entertainment together in one personal service. From shaping the idea to looking after event-day details, we help make each occasion feel special and run smoothly.

THE PERSON BEHIND THE MOMENTS

Founded by Sonia Gaikwad

Sonia is an event organiser and coordinator, a professional ZIN™ Zumba trainer, a SHIAMAK Davar Academy-certified dance trainer, and a fitness trainer. She brings event planning and a passion for movement together to create welcoming, energetic experiences.

How we can help

  • Event planning: Turn your idea into a thoughtful plan.
  • Event coordination: Keep the schedule, people, and details in sync.
  • Dance and Zumba: Add movement, music, and energy to your gathering.
  • Fitness training: Bring engaging fitness experiences to your group.
